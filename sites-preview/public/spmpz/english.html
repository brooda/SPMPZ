European towns met in Zamość to discuss a greener future

The Twin Green meeting in Zamość focused on the circular economy, sustainable energy and ways local communities can respond to environmental challenges together.

The Polish hosts welcomed representatives from Germany, Italy, the United Kingdom, Belgium, Spain and Hungary. Participants shared their experiences and explored local approaches to energy transition and the responsible use of resources.

During celebrations marking the centenary of City Park, SPMPZ members and international guests also met with the Mayor of Zamość, Rafał Zwolak, to discuss future cooperation. Visitors stayed in members’ homes, keeping the exchange grounded in direct relationships between residents.
