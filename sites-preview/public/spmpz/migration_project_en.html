About the project

The project began in 2016. It explored migration in Europe from both historical and contemporary perspectives, bringing together European, national and local points of view.

Meetings took place across the participating towns. The final evaluation event was held in Zamość in July 2018.

Evaluation and local context

Participants reviewed the results of a joint survey, attitudes towards newcomers and the impact of taking part in the project. The programme included local historians, local government representatives and visits to places connected with the region’s multi-ethnic history.

Meetings with international students, workers and volunteers connected the public debate with personal experiences of living in a new place.
